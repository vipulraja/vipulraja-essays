I'm **Vipul**. I spend my working hours building machine learning platforms, and
a good share of the rest wondering how we come to know anything at all.

I'm drawn to epistemology, theoretical and quantum physics, and the question of
what learning really is, for people and for machines. Karl Popper and David
Deutsch shaped how I think: knowledge grows through bold conjectures and honest
attempts to refute them, and good explanations are hard to vary. I try to hold
my own ideas to that standard. When I can, I build a small experiment and let
reality argue back.

**What I write about**

- **Epistemology and the growth of knowledge.** How explanations are created,
  tested, and replaced, and what that means for science, engineering, and AI.
- **Theoretical and quantum physics.** The fabric of reality, information as
  something physical, and the parts of physics that still feel like philosophy.
- **AI and machine learning.** What models actually represent, what training
  really changes, and whether anything like understanding can emerge from
  computation.
- **Running.** Long, slow miles on trails, and the clarity that shows up
  somewhere past the point where the legs want to stop.

Away from the keyboard you'll usually find me in the woods, trying to leave the
trail better than I found it, or somewhere quiet with a paperback. I like the
smell of paper more than I can justify.

These essays are where I think out loud. Some will be rigorous, some
speculative, and I'll try to be clear about which is which. Criticism is
welcome; it's how ideas get better.

*Everything here is my personal opinion and does not represent my employer.*
